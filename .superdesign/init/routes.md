# Routes (static files)

- `dist/index.html` → meta-refresh to the latest season.
- `dist/<season>/index.html` — league page: intro (h1 + one paragraph + key), grid of 30 team cards (rank, name, team fit, fill %, mini plate), "How it works" section, footer credit.
- `dist/<season>/team/<ABBR>.html` — team page: team head (h1, subline), season strip (horizontal chips for this franchise), optional caveat, two-column `.team` (left: the big plate; right: two big readings — fill % and team fit — then the player table in a panel, then the key), "How it works", footer.

Seasons 2001–2026 (end years); 30 teams per season (29 before 2005).
